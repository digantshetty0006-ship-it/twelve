    About / No. 02

    A year you can almost hold.

    Twelve levels, one dial, and a hand that keeps getting faster.

    Each level hands you a contract: a handful of months to keep. Lock them as the hand passes. Perfect timing pays more and earns mornings back. Clear the contract to unlock the next level.

    The rules change as you climb: shuffled contracts, a false light, a blinking target, a reversing hand, sudden gusts, and a narrower perfect window. The last level uses every trick at once.

    Controls

    Space, Enter or click to lock.
    R restarts the level. M toggles sound. L opens levels.

    Built with

    Hand-written SVG, vanilla JavaScript and the Web Audio API. No framework, no backend, no tracking.
