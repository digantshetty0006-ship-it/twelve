    About / No. 01

    A year you can almost hold.

    I wanted a break from dashboards. This is a small game about the only deadline everyone shares.

    Twelve months, three hundred sixty-five days, one moving hand. You cannot slow the year down. You can only learn its rhythm and put your hand on the right month.

    Controls

    Space, Enter or click to lock.
    R restarts. M toggles sound.

    Built with

    Hand-written SVG, vanilla JavaScript and the Web Audio API. No framework, no backend, no tracking.
